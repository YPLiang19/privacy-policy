隐私政策

适用于：方寸菜园 Square Inch Garden（iOS App）　生效日期：2026 年 9 月 30 日

本隐私政策说明“方寸菜园”（以下简称“本应用”）如何处理你的信息。简而言之：本应用不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人数据，你的菜园、种植、养护和收成记录只保存在你自己的设备上，开发者无法访问。

1. 我们不收集的信息

  • 本应用无需注册账号，不要求提供姓名、邮箱、手机号等任何个人信息。
  • 本应用没有自己的服务器，不包含任何第三方统计、广告、崩溃收集或追踪 SDK，也不会把你的数据发送到网络。
  • 我们不会追踪你在其他公司的 App 或网站上的活动。

2. 保存在你设备上的数据

  • 菜园数据：包括你选择的城市及其气候数据、你为阳台设置的温度和日照、菜地与容器（含补光灯设置）、种植记录、养护任务、收成记录（重量、日期）以及你为收成添加的照片，全部保存在本机的应用数据中。
  • 不进行云同步：本应用目前不会把数据同步到 iCloud 或任何服务器。删除本应用会同时删除这些数据；更换设备时，数据不会自动迁移。
  • 应用设置：例如是否开启养护提醒，保存在本机。

3. 设备权限的使用

  • 位置（仅使用 App 期间）：仅在首次设置或你在设置中轻点“使用我的位置”时获取一次，用于在本机找到离你最近的城市，以便按当地气候安排种植时间。本应用只保存你选定的城市，不保存、不上传你的位置坐标，也不会在后台获取位置。你可以拒绝授权，改为手动搜索城市。
  • 照片：通过系统照片选择器，仅读取你主动选择的那一张图片，作为收成记录的照片保存在本机。本应用无法访问你的其他照片。
  • 通知：如果你开启“养护提醒”并允许通知，本应用会在有养护任务的日子，于早上 8:00 在本机发出一条本地通知，列出当天要做的养护。通知内容在本机生成，不经过任何服务器；你可以随时在应用设置或系统设置中关闭。

4. 订阅与付款

专业版通过 Apple App Store 购买，付款、续订和退款均由 Apple 处理，适用 Apple 隐私政策。本应用只通过 Apple 的 StoreKit 在本机验证你是否拥有有效的购买，无法获取你的 Apple 账户、付款方式或账单信息。

5. 气候与城市数据

本应用内置的城市与气候数据随应用一起安装，在本机使用，不涉及你的个人信息。数据来源：城市名称与位置来自 GeoNames（CC BY 4.0）；气候常年值来自 CHELSA V2.1（CC0），并使用 NASA Langley Research Center POWER 项目的数据调整至 1991–2020 年。

6. 数据共享

由于我们不收集任何个人数据，因此不会与任何第三方共享、出租或出售你的数据。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集任何儿童的个人信息。

8. 你的权利

所有数据都保存在你的设备上，你可以随时在应用内编辑或删除任意菜地、种植和收成记录，删除本应用即会清除全部数据。由于我们不持有你的数据，无法也不需要为你提供数据查询或删除服务。

9. 政策更新

如本政策发生变更（例如将来加入云同步），我们将在本页面发布更新后的版本并修改生效日期，重大变更会在应用更新说明中提示。

10. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系我们。

Privacy Policy

Applies to: Square Inch Garden for iOS　Effective date: September 30, 2026

This policy explains how Square Inch Garden (“the App”) handles your information. In short: the App does not collect, upload, or sell any personal data. Your gardens, plantings, care tasks and harvest records stay on your own device, and we cannot access them.

1. Information we do not collect

  • No sign-up is required. The App never asks for your name, email, phone number, or any other personal information.
  • The App has no servers of its own, contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs, and does not send your data over the network.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Garden data — your chosen city and its climate figures, the temperatures and sun hours you set for a balcony, beds and containers (including grow-light settings), plantings, care tasks, harvest records (weights and dates) and any photos you add to harvests — is stored locally in the App’s data on this device.
  • No cloud sync: the App currently does not sync data to iCloud or any server. Deleting the App deletes this data, and it does not move to a new device automatically.
  • App settings, such as whether care reminders are on, are stored locally.

3. Device permissions

  • Location (while using the App): requested only once during setup, or when you tap “Use my location” in Settings, to find the nearest city on your device so planting dates match your local climate. The App keeps only the city you choose; your coordinates are never stored or uploaded, and location is never used in the background. You can decline and search for your city instead.
  • Photos: through the system photo picker, the App reads only the single image you choose and stores it on this device as a harvest photo. The App cannot access your other photos.
  • Notifications: if care reminders are on and you allow notifications, the App schedules one local notification on your device at 8:00 on each day that has care to do, listing that day’s care. Its content is generated on the device and never passes through any server; you can turn it off at any time in the App’s settings or in the system settings.

4. Subscriptions and payments

Pro is purchased through the Apple App Store. Payment, renewal, and refunds are handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. The App only uses Apple’s StoreKit on your device to check whether you own an active purchase, and has no access to your Apple Account, payment method, or billing details.

5. Climate and city data

The city and climate data are bundled with the App and used on your device; they contain no personal information. Sources: city names and locations from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0); climate normals from CHELSA V2.1 (CC0), adjusted to 1991–2020 with data from the NASA Langley Research Center POWER Project.

6. Data sharing

Because we collect no personal data, we do not share, rent, or sell any data to third parties.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

All data lives on your device. You can edit or delete any bed, planting or harvest record in the App at any time, and deleting the App removes all of it. Since we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to access or delete on your behalf.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if cloud sync is added in the future), we will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date. Significant changes will be noted in the App’s release notes.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us via GitHub Issues.
