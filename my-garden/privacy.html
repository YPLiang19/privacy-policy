隐私政策

适用于：一盆一记 Pot by Pot（iOS App 及其主屏幕小组件）　生效日期：2026 年 10 月 8 日

本隐私政策说明“一盆一记”（以下简称“本应用”）如何处理你的信息。简而言之：本应用不收集、不出售任何个人数据，你的植物档案、养护记录、照片、视频和手账保存在你自己的设备上；开启 iCloud 同步后，也会保存在你自己 iCloud 账户的私有空间里。开发者无法访问这些数据。

1. 我们不收集的信息

  • 本应用无需注册账号，不要求提供姓名、邮箱、手机号等任何个人信息。
  • 本应用没有自己的服务器，不包含任何第三方统计、广告、崩溃收集或追踪 SDK。除向 Apple 天气请求所选城市的天气预报（见第 6 节），以及在你开启 iCloud 同步时把数据存入你自己的 iCloud（见第 2 节）外，本应用不会把你的任何数据发送到网络；你主动导出或分享的内容见第 3 节。
  • 我们不会追踪你在其他公司的 App 或网站上的活动。

2. 保存在你设备上的数据

  • 应用数据：包括你选择的城市、空间（如阳台、窗台）、植物档案、养护任务与完成记录、手账文字，以及你添加的照片和视频，全部保存在本机的应用私有目录中。
  • 主屏幕小组件：为了让小组件显示今日待办、天气和预警，本应用会把这些摘要写入本机上应用与小组件共用的存储区域，只在这台设备内使用。
  • 天气缓存：所选城市的天气预报和每天的天气记录保存在本机，用于显示天气、预警和病虫害提示。
  • 应用设置：例如是否开启天气预警和病虫害预警，保存在本机。
  • iCloud 同步：“设置 > iCloud 同步”默认开启。开启后，上述应用数据（城市、空间、植物档案、养护任务与记录、手账、照片与视频、心愿单与收藏）会通过 Apple 的 CloudKit 保存在你自己 iCloud 账户的私有数据库中，并在登录同一 Apple 账户的设备之间同步。这些数据由 Apple 按 Apple 隐私政策存储和保护，占用你的 iCloud 储存空间；只有登录你 Apple 账户的设备能读取，开发者无法查看或下载。为了及时收到其他设备上的修改，本应用会注册 Apple 推送通知服务，这类静默通知只提示“有新变化”，不含你的花园内容，也不会显示在屏幕上。小组件摘要、天气缓存和应用设置不会同步。
  • 关闭或删除同步数据：你可以随时在应用设置中关闭 iCloud 同步，关闭后不再上传新的修改，本机数据不受影响。如需删除已存到 iCloud 的数据，可在 iPhone“设置 > [你的名字] > iCloud > 管理账户储存空间 > 一盆一记”中删除；删除后本应用会自动关闭同步，不会把数据重新上传。没有开启同步时，数据只在本机，删除本应用会同时删除这些数据；系统的设备备份（iCloud 云备份或电脑备份）是否包含这些数据，取决于你的系统设置。

3. 你主动导出或分享的内容

你可以在设置中“导出完整备份”，备份文件包含植物、空间、任务、手账与照片，未加密，保存或分享到哪里由你自己决定。分享给第三方或存到其他云服务后，适用该服务的隐私规则。从备份恢复时，备份文件只在本机读取。

问问 AI：本应用不接入任何 AI 或大模型服务。使用“问问 AI”时，本应用在本机把这一盆植物的名字与种类、位置、盆器与配土、备注、最近的养护和用药记录、接下来的安排、最近的手账、你所在城市及近两周的天气，以及你写下的问题整理成一段文字，并可按你的选择附上最近几张照片。发出前你可以预览全部内容；只有当你通过系统分享选择某个应用（或拷贝文字后自行粘贴）时，这些内容才会离开设备，发送给你选择的应用，之后适用该应用的隐私规则。我们无法获取这些内容。

4. 设备权限的使用

  • 位置（仅使用 App 期间）：仅在选择城市时轻点“使用我的位置”时获取一次，用于在本机找到离你最近的城市，以便按当地气候安排养护和获取天气。本应用只保存你选定的城市，不保存、不上传你的位置坐标，也不会在后台获取位置。你可以拒绝授权，改为手动搜索城市。
  • 相机：用于给植物和手账拍照或录制视频，拍摄的内容保存在本机；开启 iCloud 同步时也会存到你自己的 iCloud（见第 2 节）。
  • 麦克风：只用于录制手账视频时的声音。
  • 照片：通过系统照片选择器，仅读取你主动选择的照片或视频，保存一份副本到本机。本应用无法访问你的其他照片。
  • 添加到相册：仅在你全屏查看手账照片、长按并选择保存时，把这张照片存入你的相册；本应用不会读取相册内容。
  • 通知：你为养护任务打开提醒并允许通知后，本应用会在任务设定的时间在本机发出提醒。开启“天气预警”后，会在低温霜冻、骤然降温、酷热、暴雨、大风或台风等天气来临前的傍晚 18:00 提醒；开启“病虫害预警”后，当天气有利于你所养植物上的某种病虫害时，也会在 18:00 提醒，同一种病虫害 7 天内最多提醒一次。这些通知都在本机根据已下载的天气预报生成，不经过任何服务器，你可以随时在应用设置或系统设置中关闭。

5. 会员与付款

会员通过 Apple App Store 购买，付款、续订和退款均由 Apple 处理，适用 Apple 隐私政策。本应用只通过 Apple 的 StoreKit 在本机验证你是否拥有有效的购买，无法获取你的 Apple 账户、付款方式或账单信息。

6. 天气数据（Apple 天气）

本应用使用 Apple 的 WeatherKit 服务获取天气预报。请求时只发送你所选城市的经纬度（来自内置城市列表），不发送你设备的位置，也不附带任何能识别你身份的信息；预报通常每天更新一次，每台设备每天最多请求 15 次。为了在小组件上显示 Apple 天气标识，本应用还会从 Apple 的服务器下载该标识图片。天气数据由 Apple 天气提供，Apple 对请求的处理适用 Apple 隐私政策，数据来源见 Apple 天气数据来源。我们无法获取这些请求。

7. 内置资料与外部链接

植物图鉴、种植知识、城市与气候数据随应用一起安装，在本机使用，不涉及你的个人信息。城市名称与位置来自 GeoNames（CC BY 4.0）；气候常年值来自 CHELSA V2.1（CC0），并使用 NASA Langley Research Center POWER 项目的数据调整至 1991–2020 年。图鉴中的照片来源和参考资料链接会在你轻点后用浏览器打开，打开后适用对应网站的隐私规则。

8. 数据共享

由于我们不收集任何个人数据，因此不会与任何第三方共享、出租或出售你的数据。

9. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集任何儿童的个人信息。

10. 你的权利

所有数据都保存在你的设备上，以及（开启同步时）你自己的 iCloud 账户中。你可以随时在应用内编辑、删除任意植物、任务和手账，删除会同步到你的其他设备；也可以导出完整备份。删除本应用会清除本机上的数据，iCloud 中的数据可按第 2 节的方法删除。由于我们不持有你的数据，无法也不需要为你提供数据查询或删除服务。

11. 政策更新

如本政策发生变更，我们将在本页面发布更新后的版本并修改生效日期，重大变更会在应用更新说明中提示。

12. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系我们。

Privacy Policy

Applies to: Pot by Pot for iOS and its Home Screen widget　Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy explains how Pot by Pot (“the App”) handles your information. In short: the App does not collect or sell any personal data. Your plant records, care history, photos, videos and journal are stored on your own device and, if iCloud Sync is on, in the private area of your own iCloud account. We cannot access them.

1. Information we do not collect

  • No sign-up is required. The App never asks for your name, email, phone number, or any other personal information.
  • The App has no servers of its own and contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs. Apart from asking Apple Weather for your chosen city’s forecast (see section 6) and, with iCloud Sync on, storing your data in your own iCloud (see section 2), the App sends none of your data over the network; for content you export or share yourself, see section 3.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

2. Data stored on your device

  • App data — your chosen city, spaces (such as a balcony or windowsill), plant records, care tasks and their history, journal text, and the photos and videos you add — is stored in the App’s private storage on this device.
  • Home Screen widget: so the widget can show today’s care, the weather and alerts, the App writes a summary of them to storage shared by the App and its widget on this device. It never leaves the device.
  • Weather cache — the forecast and each day’s weather for your chosen city — is stored on this device to show the weather, warnings and pest and disease alerts.
  • App settings, such as whether weather and pest and disease alerts are on, are stored locally.
  • iCloud Sync: Settings > iCloud Sync is on by default. When it is on, the app data above (city, spaces, plant records, care tasks and history, journal, photos and videos, wishlist and favourites) is stored with Apple’s CloudKit in the private database of your own iCloud account and kept in sync across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple stores and protects it under the Apple Privacy Policy, and it counts toward your iCloud storage. Only devices signed in to your Apple Account can read it; we cannot view or download it. To hear about changes made on your other devices promptly, the App registers for Apple Push Notification service; these silent notifications only say that something changed, carry none of your garden data, and never appear on screen. The widget summary, weather cache and App settings are not synced.
  • Turning sync off or deleting synced data: you can turn iCloud Sync off in the App’s settings at any time; new changes then stay on the device and the data already there is unaffected. To delete the data stored in iCloud, go to iPhone Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Pot by Pot. After that the App turns sync off and does not upload the data again. Without sync, your data is only on this device and deleting the App deletes it; whether your system device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) includes it depends on your system settings.

3. What you export or share

You can use “Export full backup” in Settings. The backup file contains your plants, spaces, tasks, journal and photos, is not encrypted, and is saved or shared wherever you choose. Once you share it with a third party or store it in another cloud service, that service’s privacy rules apply. When you restore from a backup, the file is read only on your device.

Ask AI: the App does not connect to any AI or large-language-model service. When you use “Ask AI”, the App writes up, on your device, one plant’s name and species, location, pot and soil mix, notes, recent care and treatment records, upcoming tasks, recent journal entries, your city and its last two weeks of weather, and the question you type, and can attach a few recent photos if you choose. You can preview everything before it is sent. It leaves your device only when you pick an app in the share sheet (or copy the text and paste it yourself), goes only to the app you choose, and that app’s privacy rules then apply. We have no access to this content.

4. Device permissions

  • Location (while using the App): requested only when you tap “Use my location” while choosing a city, once, to find the nearest city on your device so care timing and weather match your local climate. The App keeps only the city you choose; your coordinates are never stored or uploaded, and location is never used in the background. You can decline and search for your city instead.
  • Camera: takes photos and videos of your plants and journal; they are stored on this device and, with iCloud Sync on, in your own iCloud (see section 2).
  • Microphone: used only to record sound in journal videos.
  • Photos: through the system photo picker, the App reads only the photos or videos you choose and keeps a copy on this device. The App cannot access your other photos.
  • Add to Photos: used only when you long-press a journal photo in full-screen view and choose to save it, to add that photo to your library. The App does not read your library.
  • Notifications: if you turn on a reminder for a care task and allow notifications, the App notifies you on this device at the time you set. With “Weather alerts” on, it notifies you at 18:00 in the evening before frost or cold, a sharp drop in temperature, extreme heat, heavy rain, strong wind or a typhoon; with “Pest and disease alerts” on, it also notifies you at 18:00 when the weather favours a pest or disease on the plants you grow, no more than once a week for each. All of these are generated on the device from the downloaded forecast and never pass through any server; you can turn them off at any time in the App’s settings or in the system settings.

5. Membership and payments

Membership is purchased through the Apple App Store. Payment, renewal, and refunds are handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. The App only uses Apple’s StoreKit on your device to check whether you own an active purchase, and has no access to your Apple Account, payment method, or billing details.

6. Weather data (Apple Weather)

The App uses Apple’s WeatherKit service for the weather forecast. Each request sends only the latitude and longitude of the city you chose (from the bundled city list) — never your device’s location — and nothing that identifies you. The forecast is usually updated once a day, and never requested more than 15 times a day on one device. To show the Apple Weather mark on the widget, the App also downloads that image from Apple’s servers. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather, and Apple handles the requests under the Apple Privacy Policy; see Apple Weather data sources. We have no access to these requests.

7. Bundled content and external links

The plant catalog, growing guides, and city and climate data are bundled with the App and used on your device; they contain no personal information. City names and locations come from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0); climate normals from CHELSA V2.1 (CC0), adjusted to 1991–2020 with data from the NASA Langley Research Center POWER Project. Photo sources and reference links in the catalog open in your browser when you tap them, and the linked website’s privacy rules then apply.

8. Data sharing

Because we collect no personal data, we do not share, rent, or sell any data to third parties.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your rights

All data lives on your device and, with sync on, in your own iCloud account. You can edit or delete any plant, task or journal entry in the App at any time, and deletions sync to your other devices; you can also export a full backup. Deleting the App removes the data from this device; data in iCloud can be deleted as described in section 2. Since we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to access or delete on your behalf.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date. Significant changes will be noted in the App’s release notes.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us via GitHub Issues.
